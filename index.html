01The show floor

The booth was never the problem.

You already spend upward of thirty thousand dollars to stand in a hall for three days. Space, build, freight, drayage, flights, shipping the rolls home again. And the buyers walking past are not walking past because the booth is too small.

They are walking past because what is on the table looks like everything else on the table. A swatch they have held before, in a box they cannot tell from the next one, beside a sheet that does not answer the question they came to ask.

Same budget Three days

A booth is rented attention. It ends Friday afternoon and you ship the samples home in the same cases they arrived in.

Same budget Every day after

A brand, a box, a data sheet, a swatch book and a set of certified settings work in every email, every distributor meeting and every show you attend from then on.

Keep exhibiting. Just stop letting the booth be the whole budget, and give the people who do stop at it something worth remembering. A designed booth and an undesigned one cost about the same to build. The only difference is whether anyone thought about it first.
